Pages and activity: every surface its own thread, one shared trunk

The person and Exo work on a surface: one or more pages and one input, with no separate chat. Everything is a page made of general blocks, and a surface's activity is its thread: everything sent, pressed, said, versioned and committed on that surface, across all of its pages. Exo gives each new topic a new page, and the person keeps the pages worth coming back to; the pages show the work now, and the activity shows how it got here. Parallel work is another surface, and what each surface decides reaches every other surface through one shared trunk of commits, so every surface feels like the same Exo.

A commit is a one-line record Exo writes to the trunk when a surface decides or finishes something, for example "Weekend trip: booked the lake cabin for Friday and Saturday".

Vocabulary follows the glossary: Exo tab, settings, surface, Home, page, message page, kept page, page strip, page history, stage, version, follow mode, sidebar, Needs you, Working, tree, settle, child surface, block, Exo block, input, activity, thread, trunk and commit. A thread and its surface are the same thing; the person-facing word is surface.

User stories

As a person, I work with Exo on a surface with one input that is always there and no chat beside it, so the work we are on is always what I see.

As a person, I read Exo's latest words on the page itself, in one Exo block, so its judgement sits with the work instead of in a transcript.

As a person, I open a surface's activity and see how its pages got here, so the pages can stay present tense without losing their past.

As a person, I start new work with the one New surface button and never make pages myself, so starting is one press and Exo shapes the pages.

As a person, I see Exo give each new topic its own page, on every surface including Home, so a topic change never overwrites the page I was on.

As a person, when I raise a topic that falls entirely outside a surface's scope, Exo offers to start a new surface or take it to Home, so surfaces stay focused instead of drifting.

As a person, while I am following, Exo's new page comes into view on its own, and once I scroll, click or type my view stays put, so Exo never pulls me away from what I am reading.

As a person, I work on several surfaces at once, with Exo working on each, and Exo never switches the surface I am in, so one topic never waits for another.

As a person, my sidebar shows Home, the surfaces that need me, and the tree of all my surfaces, so I see what needs me without managing a list of chats.

As a person, I nest, move, rename and settle my surfaces in a tree that Exo never rearranges, so the structure is mine.

As a person, every surface shows its own name at the top, Exo names a new surface from my first message, and I can rename it any time, so I know where I am without naming things first.

As a person, while Exo works I see one short line of what it is doing right now, so I can tell it is making progress and on what.

As a person, I keep the pages I come back to, and Exo refreshes a kept page in place instead of adding pages, so the page strip holds what matters.

As a person, I move a kept page into its own surface when it needs its own conversation.

As a person, I find Exo as one tab in BB, named Exo, holding my surfaces, so there is one place to work with Exo.

As a person, I open Exo's settings from the Exo tab to see how Exo is running, so that detail is there when I need it and out of my way when I work.

As a person installing Exo, the Exo tab sets Exo up the first time I open it, so first run needs no other tab.

As Exo, every turn on every surface starts from the same trunk, a child surface's turn also starts from its parent surface's pages as they are now, and a parent surface knows what its children committed, so every surface is the same Exo and no surface reads another surface's half-formed thinking.

As Exo, when a surface decides or finishes something, I commit one line to the trunk, linked to where it was said, so every other surface knows it at its next turn.

Acceptance criteria

Given a person on a page, When the person types a message in the input and sends it, then waits for Exo to finish its turn, Then the page's Exo block shows Exo's reply, the input is still shown and empty, and the surface shows no chat transcript.

Given a person on a page whose Exo block shows Exo's reply to an earlier message, When the person sends a new message on the same topic, then waits for Exo to finish its turn, Then the Exo block shows only Exo's reply to the new message.

Given a person on a surface Exo has answered once, When the person sends a second message, then waits for Exo to finish its turn, then opens the surface's activity, Then the activity lists both of the person's messages and both of Exo's replies, in the order they were said.

Given a person on a page with a button, When the person presses the button, then opens the surface's activity, Then the activity lists the press by the button's label, with the values it sent.

Given a person on a surface Exo has answered once, When the person types a message in the input without sending it, then opens the surface's activity, Then the activity lists the earlier message and Exo's reply, and nothing of the unsent text.

Given a person on a page Exo has changed twice, When the person opens the surface's activity, then opens the page's earlier version from it, Then the page shows as it was at that version, marked as an earlier version.

Given a person on a surface where Exo committed a decision, When the person opens the surface's activity, Then the activity shows the commit as one line, marked as a commit, next to the Exo turn it came from.

Given a person on a surface, When the person presses the New surface button, Then a new surface with one empty page is in view, with the input shown and empty, and the sidebar lists the new surface.

Given a person on a surface other than Home whose page in view is about one topic, When the person sends a message about another topic within the surface's scope, then waits without scrolling, clicking or typing until Exo finishes its turn, Then a new page about that topic is in view, and the page strip lists it as the surface's current page.

Given a person on Home whose page in view is about one topic, When the person sends a message about an unrelated topic, then waits without scrolling, clicking or typing until Exo finishes its turn, Then Home shows a new page about that topic in view, with no offer to start a new surface.

Given a person on a surface whose page in view is a message page, When the person sends a message about another topic, then waits until Exo finishes its turn, then opens the surface's page history from the page strip, then opens the earlier page from it, Then the earlier page is in view as Exo last left it.

Given a person on a surface other than Home that is about one project, When the person sends a message about a topic entirely outside that project, then waits until Exo finishes its turn, Then the page in view offers to start a new surface for the topic and to take it to Home.

Given a person on a surface other than Home that is about one project, When the person sends a message about a topic entirely outside that project, then waits until Exo finishes its turn, then presses the offer to start a new surface, Then a new surface with a page about the topic is in view, and the tree lists it under the surface it came from.

Given a person on a surface other than Home that is about one project, When the person sends a message about a topic entirely outside that project, then waits until Exo finishes its turn, then presses the offer to take it to Home, Then Home is in view with a page about the topic.

Given a person on a surface, When the person sends a message about another topic, then scrolls the page in view before Exo finishes its turn, then waits until Exo finishes, Then the page the person scrolled is still in view, and the page strip shows a dot on the new page.

Given a person on a surface, When the person sends a message about another topic, then scrolls the page in view before Exo finishes its turn, then waits until Exo finishes, then sends a message about a third topic, then waits without scrolling, clicking or typing until Exo finishes its turn, Then the page about the third topic is in view.

Given a person on a page Exo has answered, scrolled down to a button below the first screen, When the person presses the button, then waits without scrolling, clicking or typing until Exo finishes its turn, Then the top of the page is in view, under the surface's name, showing the button's label as the person's bubble and Exo's reply to the press as plain text below it.

Given a person with two surfaces, When the person sends a message on one surface, then opens the other surface before Exo finishes that turn, then waits until Exo finishes its turn on the first surface, Then the surface the person opened is still the surface in view, not pulled back to the first surface.

Given a person with two surfaces, When the person sends a message on one surface, then opens the other surface before Exo finishes that turn, then waits until Exo finishes its turn on the first surface, Then the sidebar lists the first surface under Needs you.

Given a person with two surfaces, When the person sends a message on one surface, then opens the other surface before Exo finishes that turn, Then the sidebar shows the first surface in the Working group.

Given a person with two surfaces, When the person sends a message on one surface, then opens the other surface before Exo finishes that turn, then sends a message there, then goes back to the first surface after Exo finishes its turn there, Then the Exo block on the first surface's page in view shows Exo's reply to the first message.

Given a person with Home and several other surfaces, one of which is waiting on the person, When the person opens the sidebar, Then the sidebar shows Home first, then Needs you with the waiting surface, then the tree of every surface, and it lists no pages.

Given a person with a surface under Needs you, When the person settles that surface, Then the sidebar no longer lists it under Needs you, and the tree still lists it where it was.

Given a person whose surfaces are all settled or working, When the person opens the sidebar, Then Needs you shows that nothing needs the person.

Given a person on a surface, When the person presses the New surface button, Then the tree lists the new surface at its top level.

Given a person with two surfaces at the top level of the tree, When the person drags one surface onto the other, Then the tree lists the dragged surface nested under the other.

Given a person with a surface nested under another surface, When the person sends a message on the nested surface, then waits until Exo finishes its turn, Then the tree still lists the surface under the surface the person put it under, with the name the person gave it.

Given a person on a surface with a message page in view, When the person presses Keep on that page, then sends a message about another topic, then waits until Exo finishes its turn, Then the page strip lists the kept page, marked as kept, and the new page.

Given a person on a surface with a kept page in view, When the person asks Exo to update the kept page, then waits until Exo finishes its turn, Then the kept page shows the update, and the page strip lists it once, with no added page.

Given a person on a surface with a kept page Exo has refreshed once, When the person opens the kept page's history, then opens its earlier version, Then the kept page shows as it was before the refresh, marked as an earlier version.

Given a person on a surface with a kept page that is not the page in view, When the person unkeeps that page, Then the page strip no longer lists it and its page history lists it.

Given a person on a surface with two kept pages, When the person drags one kept page past the other in the page strip, Then the page strip lists the two kept pages in the new order.

Given a person on Home with a kept page, When the person moves the kept page into its own surface, Then a new surface holding that page is in view, and the tree lists the new surface.

Given Exo on a child surface whose parent surface's pages have changed since the child's last turn, When the person sends a message on the child surface, Then Exo's turn reads the parent surface's pages as they are now and nothing of the parent's activity; no screen shows it.

Given Exo on a surface, while another surface that is neither its parent nor its child has committed a decision, When the person sends a message on the first surface, Then Exo's turn reads that commit line and nothing of the other surface's activity; no screen shows it.

Given Exo on a parent surface whose child surface has committed a decision, When the person sends a message on the parent surface, Then Exo's turn reads that commit line, marked as coming from that child surface, and nothing of the child's activity; no screen shows it.

Given Exo on a surface that decides something in a turn, When the turn ends, Then the trunk holds one new commit line for it, linked to that turn in the surface's activity, and nothing else from the turn; no screen shows it.

Given a person on a surface Exo has answered once, When the person sends a second message, then waits for Exo to finish its turn, Then the top of the page in view shows, under the surface's name, the second message as the person's bubble, Exo's reply to it as plain text below the bubble and not in a box, then the page's content, and nothing of the first message.

Given a person on a page whose content is taller than the window, When the person scrolls the page down, Then the input stays over the bottom of the window at the width of the page's content, and the content scrolling beneath it fades out from the input's middle down.

Given a person on a surface whose page in view is about one topic, When the person sends a message about another topic, then waits without scrolling, clicking or typing until Exo finishes its turn, Then the page about the new topic fills the surface on its own, and the earlier page shows only as a pill in the page strip, not beside it.

Given a person with a surface named "Garden plan", When the person opens that surface, Then the top of the surface shows "Garden plan" as its title, not "Exosurface".

Given a person on a new surface named "New surface", When the person sends a first message about planning a garden, then waits for Exo to finish its turn, Then the surface's title and its entry in the sidebar show a short name about the garden instead of "New surface".

Given a person on a surface Exo has named, When the person renames it to "Backyard", then sends a message, then waits for Exo to finish its turn, Then the surface's title and its entry in the sidebar still show "Backyard".

Given a person on a surface, When the person sends a message Exo needs several steps to answer, such as reading a note and then searching the web, then waits while Exo works, Then the Exo block shows one line naming what Exo is doing at that moment, such as "Reading the garden note", and the line changes as Exo moves to its next step.

Given a person on a surface whose Exo block shows what Exo is doing, When the person waits until Exo finishes its turn, Then the Exo block shows Exo's reply and no activity line.

Given a person with Exo set up, When the person opens BB, Then BB's navigation shows one tab for Exo, named "Exo", and no tab named "Exosurface".

Given a person with Exo set up, When the person opens the Exo tab, Then the sidebar and Home are in view, with the input shown.

Given a person with Exo installed, When the person opens BB's list of installed plugins, Then it lists Exo's plugin as "Exo".

Given a person with Exo set up, When the person opens the Exo tab, then presses Settings, Then settings show that Exo is set up, the model Exo runs on, whether Exo is running, and the owner profile Exo works as, and no control for testing Exo.

Given a person on a surface other than Home, When the person presses Settings, then closes settings, Then that surface is in view, with its page as the person left it.

Given a person with Exo installed and not yet set up, When the person opens the Exo tab, Then settings are in view, offering to set Exo up, with no surface and no input.

Given a person with Exo installed and not yet set up, When the person opens the Exo tab, then presses Set up Exo, then waits until set-up finishes, Then the sidebar and Home are in view, with the input shown and empty.

Modular boundaries

Pages own every page's blocks, its versions, its kind (message or kept), the surface it is on, the order of kept pages, and each surface's page history. Caller-facing seam: the page tools Exo calls on the pages of the surface its turn is on, and the pages the surface view renders. Dependency direction: self-contained; surfaces, activity and the surface view depend on pages, and pages depend on none of them. Observable scope: what a page shows, its versions, and what the page strip and page history list.

Surfaces own every surface, Home included, the tree of parent and child surfaces, names, and whether a surface is working, needs the person, or is settled. Caller-facing seam: the person's nest, move, rename and settle, Exo's adding of a child surface, and the state each surface reports as its turns start and end. Dependency direction: self-contained; the surface view and activity depend on surfaces, and surfaces depend on neither. Observable scope: the sidebar's Home, Needs you, Working and tree.

Activity owns each surface's thread: the record of what was sent, pressed, said, versioned and committed on that surface, across all of its pages. Caller-facing seam: one activity per surface, written by the person's sends and presses and by Exo's turns on that surface, read by the activity view and by Exo's turns on that surface only. Dependency direction: external provider OptChat, as each surface's own conversation; an activity points at page versions and writes commits to the trunk, and no activity reads another. Observable scope: the activity view of a surface, and the Exo block on each page, which shows the latest Exo turn there.

The trunk owns the shared record of commits for all of the person's surfaces. Caller-facing seam: a commit appends one line linked to its activity entry; every Exo turn reads the trunk. Dependency direction: external provider OptChat, as one conversation that holds only commits, so it stays readable as it grows; activities depend on the trunk, and it depends on no surface. Observable scope: the commit lines Exo reads each turn, and commits shown in a surface's activity.

The surface view owns what the person sees and does: the Exo tab, the sidebar, the surface in view, its page in view, the page strip, follow mode, the input, the New surface button and the activity view. Caller-facing seam: a send or press goes to the activity of the surface it was made on. Dependency direction: self-contained within the package; it depends on surfaces, pages, activity and settings, and nothing depends on the surface view. Observable scope: everything on screen except the settings screen.

Settings own Exo's set-up and what the person sees of how Exo runs: whether Exo is set up, the model it runs on, whether it is running, and the owner profile it works as. Caller-facing seam: the settings screen the surface view opens, the set-up the person starts there, and the status Exo's owner reports. Dependency direction: external provider, Exo's owner (Pi with OptChat), which sets up and reports its own runtime and profile; the surface view depends on settings, and settings depend on no surface. Observable scope: the settings screen, and whether the Exo tab opens on settings or on Home.

Surfaces

S1 A surface holds one or more pages and shows its page in view and one input; it has no chat column and no transcript.

S2 The input is always shown on a surface in view, and what the person sends from it is a turn in the activity of the surface in view.

S3 A press of a button on a page is a turn in the activity of that page's surface.

S4 Exo makes every page: on every surface, Home included, a new topic gets a new page; the person never makes a page, and asks Exo instead.

S5 When a topic falls entirely outside the scope of a surface other than Home, Exo offers, on the page, to start a new surface for it or to take it to Home, rather than drift; Home has no scope and never offers.

S6 Exo never switches which surface the person is in; a surface the person is not in may edit its blocks and change its pages quietly, and lights up in the sidebar.

S7 New surface is the only button for starting new work, and there is no New page button; New surface starts a top-level surface with one empty page and puts it in view.

S8 A surface Exo starts, from an offer the person presses or a kept page the person moves, is a child of the surface it came from; one that comes from Home is at the top level of the tree.

S9 Exo works on several surfaces at the same time; a turn on one surface never waits for a turn on another.

S10 Every surface is worked on by the same Exo, with the same identity, vault and trunk.

S11 The input floats over the bottom of the surface at the width of the page's content, not the full window; the page scrolls beneath it, and content fades out from the input's middle down.

S12 The top of a surface shows the surface's name as its title, never the product's name.

S13 A surface shows one page at a time; its other pages show only as pills in the page strip, never beside the page in view.

Voice and images in the input, and the surface on other devices.

Exo starting a surface without an offer the person presses.

Follow mode

F1 The person is following from each send or button press until they next scroll, click or type on that surface; the press itself does not end following.

F6 Each send or button press puts the top of the page in view, so the person's bubble and Exo's reply are the first thing they see; a press shows as the person's bubble with the button's label.

F2 While the person is following, Exo moving to a new page puts that page in view.

F3 Once the person scrolls, clicks or types, the view stays on the page they are on, and each new page Exo moves to gets a dot in the page strip until the person opens it.

F4 The person's next send resumes following.

F5 Follow mode moves the view only between pages of the surface the person is in, never to another surface.

The sidebar

N1 The sidebar lists surfaces only, never pages, in three parts: Home, always at the top; Needs you; and the tree.

N2 Needs you lists the surfaces, other than the one the person is in, that have finished a turn or wait on the person; a surface rises into it on its own and leaves it when the person settles it, and Needs you may be empty.

N3 A surface Exo is working on sits in a folded Working group beside Needs you until it finishes or needs the person, then rises into Needs you; wherever else the sidebar lists a working surface, it shows as active.

N4 Home stays at the top and lights up in its place when it is working or needs the person.

N5 The tree lists every surface, nested like Notion pages; the person nests, moves, renames and settles surfaces, and Exo may add a child surface but never moves, nests, renames or settles one, except the one name N7 gives a new surface.

N7 A new surface is "New surface" until its first turn; then a small, fast model names it once from the person's first message, and from Exo's reply when it has arrived. The naming model is set once in Exosurface's configuration and shared by every profile. A surface the person has renamed is never named again by Exo.

N6 A settled surface leaves Needs you and keeps its place in the tree; it rises into Needs you again the next time it finishes a turn or waits on the person.

The Exo tab and settings

T1 Exo is one tab in BB, named Exo: its surfaces, and the memory behind them; no other tab shows either, and BB names the plugin Exo.

T2 Once Exo is set up, the Exo tab opens on the sidebar and Home.

T3 Below the tree, the sidebar has one Settings button; it is not a surface, and it opens settings in place of the surface in view, beside the sidebar.

T4 Settings show whether Exo is set up, the model it runs on, whether it is running, and the owner profile it works as; they have no input, and closing them, or opening a surface from the sidebar, puts that surface back in view as the person left it.

T5 Until Exo is set up, the Exo tab shows only settings, offering to set Exo up: they show what Exo will use, each filled in where Exo can find it, the person may change any of them, and Set up Exo sets Exo up once; a set-up that stops part way resumes from settings.

T6 Exo carries nothing that exists only to test it, such as test buttons, an offline test model or proof views; tests bring their own.

Pages

P1 A page is made of general blocks from the visual catalog; no page or block is specific to plans, weeks or any other domain.

P2 Every page is on exactly one surface and keeps its own versions, so it can be rewound; its conversation belongs to the surface's activity.

P3 A surface lists its pages only in its own page strip, never in the sidebar: its kept pages in the person's order and its current message page, with the surface's page history one click away.

P4 A page is a message page unless it is kept: a snapshot Exo changes only while it is the topic, which slides into the surface's page history when the topic moves, one click back.

P5 A page becomes a kept page when the person presses Keep on it, either unprompted or when Exo suggests keeping it; a kept page stays in the page strip, Exo refreshes it in place, and its earlier versions live in that page's history, never as extra pages.

P6 The person can unkeep a kept page, which makes it a message page again and, when it is not the topic, slides it into the page history; the person can also reorder kept pages in the page strip.

P7 The person can move a kept page into its own surface when it needs its own conversation; the new surface holds that page with its versions, and the page leaves the old surface's page strip. For example, a "Today" agenda starts as a kept page in Home and moves to its own Daily surface when it needs one.

P8 A surface's pages and its activity are one record with two views: the pages show it now, and the activity shows how it got here.

The Exo block

E1 Every page has one Exo block, which shows Exo's words from its latest turn on that page.

E2 Each Exo turn replaces the block's words; earlier words stay in the surface's activity as the turns they belong to.

E3 A turn that only changes the page leaves the Exo block empty, so no earlier words sit beside a changed page.

E4 While Exo works on the page, the Exo block says so.

E5 The Exo block carries what the page cannot, such as judgement or a caveat, and never repeats what the page shows.

E6 When Exo needs something from the person, the page's own inputs ask for it, and the Exo block only points to them.

E7 Only Exo writes the Exo block; the person answers through the input.

E8 The Exo block opens the page in view, under the surface's title: the person's latest message as a bubble, then Exo's reply as plain text, not a callout or a card, then the page's content; it scrolls with the page and shows no earlier turns, which stay in the activity.

E9 While Exo works, the Exo block shows one line of what Exo is doing at that moment, from the model's own reasoning summary when the provider streams one and otherwise from the step it is taking, replaced as Exo goes and gone when the reply arrives.

Non-goals

Enforcing the steps of parallel work: surfaces and their pages are the interaction layer, and scripted workflows belong to Exomachina underneath.

Renaming the plugin's internal ids, Exo's page tools or its provider: only the names the person sees become Exo.

Page bounds and the snapshot format, which page bounds owns.
